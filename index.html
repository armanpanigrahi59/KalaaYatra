KalaaYatra

Five thousand years of Indian art, told through the objects that carried it — from a bronze dancer in Mohenjo-daro to a canvas in a Mumbai studio. Follow the river below, and stop wherever a piece catches your eye.

Begin the journey

भarat's art was never one thing. It was seals pressed into clay by a river civilization, stone lions carved to carry an emperor's word, bronze gods caught mid-dance, miniatures painted with a single squirrel-hair brush, and protest folded into a Kalighat sketch. Each era answered the one before it. KalaaYatra lays eight of these eras end to end — click any artifact below to step closer.
